How this works

Latency, download, and upload are measured against speed.cloudflare.com — Cloudflare's CORS-enabled speedtest endpoints. Network metadata comes from speed.cloudflare.com/meta. Everything else (browser, connection type) is read from the page locally; no third-party analytics are loaded.

Results reflect what the browser can do over a small number of parallel HTTPS connections. Real link capacity is usually higher than what a single browser tab can saturate.
